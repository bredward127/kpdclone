# KDP publishing metadata and AI disclosure notes

Reviewed 2026-08-27 from official KDP help pages.

- KDP requires authors/publishers/selling partners to inform KDP of AI-generated text, images, or translations when publishing or republishing. AI-assisted content does not require disclosure under the cited distinction.
- KDP says the creator remains responsible for content-guideline and intellectual-property compliance.
- KDP metadata guidance covers title, subtitle, series, description, author/contributors, keywords, audience, and publisher/imprint fields.
- KDP recommends up to seven keywords or short phrases; keywords must accurately describe the book and must not mislead or use unauthorized brands/authors.
- KDP states that each print format/new edition needs the appropriate ISBN treatment, and owned-ISBN imprint details must match the ISBN agency record.
